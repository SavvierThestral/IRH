Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2025

1. Who We Are

IRH - Iberian Riding Horses is an equestrian brokerage based in San Pedro del Pinatar, Murcia, Spain. We operate the website iberianridinghorses.com and handle inquiries related to the sale and acquisition of horses.

2. Information We Collect

When you contact us through our website or by email, we may collect:

  • Your name and surname
  • Your email address and phone number
  • Information about your equestrian experience and preferences
  • Any other details you voluntarily provide in your message

We do not collect payment information through this website.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide exclusively to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and assess your needs
  • Suggest suitable horses from our current selection
  • Coordinate visits, trials, and purchase processes

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, or as required by applicable law. If you wish us to delete your data, please contact us at info@iberianridinghorses.com.

5. Cookies

This website uses only essential functional storage (localStorage) to remember your language preference (Spanish or English). No tracking cookies or third-party analytics are used.

6. Your Rights

Under applicable data protection law, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at info@iberianridinghorses.com.
